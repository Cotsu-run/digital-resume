Selected Works

Machine Learning

OCR & QA Platform

An automated solution for comparing packaging artwork, detecting differences across text, visual, and color elements, and supporting faster, more reliable artwork quality assurance.

View project

Detect errors in artwork before production

Context

A packaging team signing off artwork revisions by eye — every new proof read line by line against the approved file, under a print deadline.

What was needed

Each round had to be checked for changed wording, missing elements, colour shifts and spacing drift. Done by hand it is slow, and what slips through tends to be small: a changed dosage, a dropped line, a logo a shade off.

Work I did

I shaped the product and built it. The comparison flow, the findings model — different, missing, added, matching, line-height — and the review screen where the two files sit side by side with every difference boxed and numbered. Under it, a Next.js app rendering PDFs to canvas, with the artwork checks and the specification panel in one workspace, in Thai and English.

What Changed

Reviewers work from one list instead of two documents, and every finding points to a spot on the artwork. An eyeball comparison became a repeatable check, and the record of what changed comes out with the file.

Finance · Mobile

Mobile Banking

A mobile banking app — secure authentication, real-time transfers, and account management. Designed for everyday transactions with a clear, low-friction flow.

View project

A convenient banking application allows all investors with the smallest possible amount.

Context

A bank opening investment products to customers who had been priced out of them — people who can set aside a little each month, not a lump sum.

What was needed

Investing had to start at an amount anyone could spare, and the app had to explain what someone was buying without handing them a prospectus. Onboarding, funding and the first order all had to hold up to the rules that come with a regulated product.

Work I did

I set the product direction and designed the flows end to end — identity checks and risk profiling, funding an account, choosing a product and placing the first order, then following it — and worked each screen through with engineering: every state, every error, and the wording, which has to be exact when money is involved.

What Changed

Opening a position takes minutes and a small amount, instead of an appointment and a minimum balance. Customers who had never held an investment product started with what they could afford, and kept adding to it from the same app they already used every day.

Web · Mobile

E-commerce

A comprehensive e-commerce platform with seamless checkout and inventory management.

View project

Mobile applications provide a seamless shopping experience, reducing wasted time and ensuring product freshness

Context

Shoppers ordering from their phones for delivery at a time they choose — food and everyday goods, where freshness is part of what they are paying for.

What was needed

Ordering had to be quick enough to do between other things: find the item, pick a delivery window, pay, done. Behind that, stock had to be accurate and the window had to be one the store and the riders could keep — a late order is a tired one.

Work I did

I owned the product side: browsing and search, the basket and checkout down to the payment step, delivery windows, and order tracking. I cut the path to a paid order to the fewest screens that still collect what a delivery needs, prioritised the build with engineering, and put it in front of real customers before it shipped.

What Changed

Ordering takes a couple of minutes instead of a phone call, customers pick the window that suits them, and orders arrive inside it — so what turns up is still fresh.
